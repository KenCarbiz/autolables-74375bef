A. Approved design changes (owner decision or standing rule)
 1. Title block: the one-Administration title block and the section row
    (owner decision).
 2. Review changes is disabled until the draft changes (the mock draws it
    enabled). Correct behaviour.
 3. The Escalation switch lives on the Escalation child page, so the
    Response card does not draw it. Correct behaviour.
 4. Truthful copy:
    - "In-app notifications - Not supported yet" (mock: "Coming soon");
    - the warning names what is missing ("Missing: BDC destination,
      escalation destinations");
    - the draft bar reads "No pending changes. Edits stay in this draft
      until you review and apply them."
    Standing rule: real data semantics.
 5. The scope box has no chevron, because routing is stored once per
    account (no scope choice exists). Real data semantics.

B. Rendering differences
 6. Inter text widths: "Customer should get a reply within:" and the timing
    hints wrap differently from the mock.
 7. Lucide glyphs against the mock's own drawings: grip, user, users,
    shield, clock, mail, bell, chevrons and chip marks.
 8. Switch knob and track rendering; 1px edge anti-aliasing where the mock
    draws soft 2px borders.
 9. The shell's child-tab row is 13px text in a 36px row (mock 15px in a
    45px row).
10. The sticky Draft configuration bar is anchored to the viewport, so in
    the frame it sits over the mock's card bottoms.
11. "DESIGN CONCEPT" chip and caption: mock-only annotations.
