# Customer Display License Matrix (Gate 1, Agent 2, directive §34)

Scope: every data family the platform stores from a provider, classified strictly from evidence in this repo. Classifications are INTERNAL USE CLEARED, CUSTOMER DISPLAY CLEARED, or UNKNOWN-REVIEW REQUIRED. No contract, terms document or provider agreement for MarketCheck, NeoVIN, Firecrawl, Black Book, VinAudit, MonroneyLabels, Google or the LLM vendors exists in the repository; the only in-repo terms text is the VINData summary in `supabase/functions/marketcheck-title-report/index.ts:6-19`. Where a family is already rendered by the LOCKED Passport it is marked "already displayed today, status unknown, in production" per the Gate 1 rule, and the owner decides. Live figures cite the queries in `PROVIDER_COST_AND_ENTITLEMENT_MAP.md` (Q-numbers) or this document's own queries (LQ-numbers, run 2026-09-09 against Harte active rows, `archived_at IS NULL`, 130 rows). Revised after cross-review 2026-09-09 and after the Gate 1 inspection (corrections listed in the final section); the twelve critical fields are mapped in section 2a. Count scope: every count in this document is the pilot tenant (Harte, `3f0f97f5-4151-4e32-88ef-e2d6fc5a3142`, 130 active rows) unless it says "all tenants"; across all tenants there are 131 published rows, the extra row being VIN `JN8AZ3DB6T9435410` in tenant `93ae75c1-2071-4a5a-95c4-6095de55fe1f` (`tenants.name` AutoLabels.io), price $171 (LQ4).

## Executive summary

1. Nothing in the repo grants redistribution rights for any paid provider family. Every MarketCheck analytical family (DOM, price change, reference price, market value, comparables, MDS, sales velocity, listing history) is UNKNOWN-REVIEW REQUIRED; the repo itself frames Black Book, `market_payload` and `comparables` as "a licensing question" (`supabase/functions/_shared/lotFeedRow.ts:28-33`) and then exempts them for the locked Passport (`lotFeedRow.ts:69-72`, `public-listing-view/index.ts:901-907`).
2. Already displayed to anonymous shoppers today from MarketCheck analytics: market value and position, days on market, comparable count, radius and median, a curated competitor list on `/v/:slug/comparables` rendered as heading, miles, price and difference only (competitor city/state, distance, drivetrain, dom and vin ship in the JSON but are not rendered: `marketcheck-comps/index.ts:141-152`, `VehiclePassportV2Detail.tsx:1395-1432`), ownership count derived from `carfax_1_owner`, listing-history-derived in-service date and owner timeline, sold-stats claims when `soldDisplayable`.
3. NOT displayed today though present: `price_change_percent` and `ref_price` (read only by the unmounted `src/components/listing/MarketTimingCard.tsx:12-13` and by `src/lib/vehiclePrefill.tsx:141`, which no sticker template renders), `ref_price_dt`, `dom_180`, `dos_active`, `in_transit`, `first_seen_at*`, `last_seen_at`, MDS as a number (`daysSupply` derived but not rendered in `VehiclePassportGoverned.tsx`), Black Book values (provider unconfigured), OEM incentives (globally off, no writer for the field the Passport reads), NMVTIS results (session-only by design), IIHS awards (permission-gated, off).
4. Factory data (NeoVIN) is displayed everywhere: specs, MSRP ladder, features and equipment, warranty program match, the AutoLabels-rendered factory sticker reproduction, and the printed window sticker / addendum / CPO sheet / trade-up generators via `useVehiclePrefill` (`src/lib/vehiclePrefill.tsx:120-150`; importers `UsedCarSticker.tsx:19`, `NewCarSticker.tsx:17`, `CpoSheet.tsx:5`, `TradeUpSticker.tsx:7`, `StickerStudioGenerator.tsx:21`, all under `src/pages/`). Raw NeoVIN payloads are persisted verbatim in `neovin_snapshots` (347 rows). Rights to persist and redisplay: UNKNOWN. Qualifier (Gate 1 inspection): the top-level `mc_attributes.engine/drivetrain` that every consumer reads are written from NeoVIN by `marketcheck-specs/index.ts:283,287` and then rebuilt from the feed `build` object by the nightly sync (`marketcheck-sync/index.ts:1137-1138`; `DECODE_OWNED_KEYS` :95-100 carries neither key), so the steady-state displayed value is the feed's, not NeoVIN's (section 2a).
5. Federal sources (NHTSA recalls and safety ratings, EPA fuel economy) are asserted in code as free public-domain data (`epa-fuel-economy/index.ts:2-3`, `trade-appraisal/index.ts:49-50`); classified CUSTOMER DISPLAY CLEARED pending owner acceptance of the basis: the legal basis is that works of the United States Government are not subject to copyright (17 U.S.C. 105); the in-repo evidence is a code comment, not a terms file, and the terms of use of `api.nhtsa.gov` and `fueleconomy.gov` (attribution, rate limits, redistribution) are not in the repo and were not retrieved -- UNKNOWN (Unknown 11). NHTSA safety ratings and complaints ARE customer-displayed: Passport owner-reviews panel (`src/components/passport/PassportPanel.tsx:1471-1491,1505-1508`) and the printed used-car sticker star block (`src/pages/UsedCarSticker.tsx:92-110`), both via `supabase/functions/nhtsa-safety`.
6. CARFAX: only the dealer's own report link is stored and shown (`_shared/carfaxLink.ts:1-13`); the one-owner badge is a MarketCheck feed flag (`carfax_1_owner` true on 57/130, LQ1) that the dealer-VDP crawler can also set with `one_owner_source: 'dealer_vdp'` (`crawl-advertised-prices/index.ts:1537-1551`; 0 rows today, LQ1). On screen it is attributed "(CARFAX)" whenever a report link exists, without consulting `one_owner_source` (`VehiclePassportGoverned.tsx:674,1425`); 12 cars carry a link, so the line can render on at most 12. Restating report findings is documented as not defensible (`AUTOLABELS_VEHICLE_TRUTH_EXPANSION_ASSESSMENT.md` §G).
7. NMVTIS/VINData: the repo records the accepted terms (no persistence beyond a session, provider-side 90-day reuse, dealer attestation is a business record). INTERNAL USE CLEARED for the session view; CUSTOMER DISPLAY of any title claim derived from it is not implemented and directive §29 forbids it without approval. The attestation column and pull ledger are absent live (Q23, Q29).
8. OEM documents: brochure and owner's-manual LINKS to manufacturer-hosted files are shown; COPIES are stored only for franchised dealers under a schema-enforced claim (`oem-document-store/index.ts:5-14`, `_shared/oemDocCopy.ts:185-191`). The franchise rule is the repo's own licensing assumption, not a manufacturer grant. The "Original OEM Window Sticker" family has no provider key, and the reproduction is titled as a factory document in two customer surfaces (assessment §K). The same documents page prints the VIN's last six characters labelled "Stock #" (`VehiclePassportDocuments.tsx:715`): a fabricated value for a critical field, CURRENT OLD VALUE WRONG, while the real stock (`vehicle_files.stock_number`, 130/130) is never attached by `public-listing-view` (section 2a).
9. Generated descriptions are the platform's own output, gated by channel policy; model-vendor output terms are not in the repo. Photos: every photo URL on the 128 active rows with photos resolves to the dealer's website or syndication CDN (Dealer.com `assets.cai-media-management.com`, HomeNet `content.homenetiol.com`, Shift Digital `*.shiftdigitalinventory.com`); 0 URLs on any marketcheck.com host (LQ2). The MarketCheck `photo_links_cached` fallback (`marketcheck-sync/index.ts:1077-1079`) is a code path only today; its rights are UNKNOWN.
10. Two sister-app endpoints ship the same `shapeLotRow` row: `autofilm-feed` and `vehicle-lookup` (`supabase/functions/vehicle-lookup/index.ts:18,289`; `x-lookup-secret` header :3,162; LIST mode returns the whole lot up to 500 rows per page, :155-156). Both withhold `blackbook`, `mc_raw`, `market_payload`, `comparables` by denylist but ship `history_payload` (scrubbed of competitor VDP URLs), `recall_payload`, `market_value`, `mc_attributes` (including `dom`, `price_change_percent`, `ref_price`, `in_transit`) and NeoVIN `build_sheet`; those are third-party redistribution to another product and are UNKNOWN.
